You can upload any file type, regardless of extension, with a few security‑related exceptions.

The following extensions are blocked for safety reasons: .exe, .scr, .cpl, .docm, .jar, .html, .htm, .sh, .bat, .cmd, .js, .vbs, .ps1, .msi, .dll, and .so. If you need to share such files, you can always pack them into an archive (e.g. .zip, .7z, .rar) and upload that instead.

That said, I reserve the right to remove any uploaded file that contains child pornography or sexualised depictions of real minors, viruses or other malware, or heavy gore – regardless of its extension.

As for size limits, the maximum file size is 100 MB on the both mare.by and temp.mare.by. Maybe in future it will be larger.

Files uploaded to mare.by are intended to be stored forever. That's the goal – once you upload something, I plan to keep it available indefinitely. Of course, it's difficult to say precisely how long "forever" will last in practice, but I'm committed to doing whatever is necessary to preserve your files for as long as possible. There are no automatic deletion timers, and I have no intention of purging content unless it violates our policies.

On temp.mare.by, files are not stored forever – instead, you choose the retention period when you upload them. The file will be automatically and permanently deleted once that time runs out. There is no automatic renewal – if you need the file longer, you'll have to re-upload it with a new expiry. This service is designed for temporary sharing, so you have full control over how long your files stay online. After expiration, the file is gone for good, so make sure to download anything important beforehand.
